COLOPHON ×
Aa IBM Plex Sans

Clean, modern aesthetic and high legibility

IBM Plex Sans balances geometric structure with humanist warmth. It complements the digital nature of this project while maintaining clarity across sizes and layouts. Its versatility made it an ideal choice for both interface elements and longer text.
